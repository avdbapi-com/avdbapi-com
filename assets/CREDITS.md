## Fonts

Text inside the SVG files is converted to outlines (paths), so no font file is loaded. The outlines come from
these fonts, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org):

- Bricolage Grotesque, Copyright 2022 The Bricolage Grotesque Project Authors

## Technology logos

The PHP, MariaDB and WordPress logos in the tech badges come from Simple Icons 16.35.0
(https://simpleicons.org, CC0 1.0). They are trademarks of their owners and are used only to name the
technology a product runs on.

## Colours

The 17 theme swatches of the JavCMS social preview are the real accent colours of the 17 JavCMS 1.2.0 themes,
measured from each theme's own stylesheets.

## Motion

Only the hero moves: one 6-second loop on a 12 fps step grid (a light sweep, the playback bar with a pulse ring
and five twinkling sparkles). It stops for visitors whose system asks for reduced motion. Everything else is
still.

## Release pulse

The release pulse image (branch "output") is drawn by ../.github/pulse/pulse.py (MIT licence, see
../.github/pulse/LICENSE) from the public GitHub API. Its text outlines come from Space Grotesk and Bricolage
Grotesque (SIL OFL 1.1, ../.github/pulse/OFL.txt).
